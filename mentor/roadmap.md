### Write, ask, and share

Add notes to your own folder in any format that works for you. Record what you learned and questions you would like to discuss, and share them with the group.

You do not need to summarize every part of the lectures. You might explain an idea in your own words, work through an example, or identify a point that you do not yet understand. Useful timestamps help others find the passage you are discussing.

**Your own thinking matters more than length.** A few thoughtful observations or questions are enough. Do not use AI to generate or pad your notes, and do not submit AI-generated summaries as your own work.

### Sharing with the group

Link to your notes from your folder's `README.md`. Use an issue or discussion if you want to bring a question or observation to everyone's attention, and link back to your notes.
