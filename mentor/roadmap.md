# Shared roadmap

For the first three months, we explore theoretical computer science (TCS) through readings and lectures. Most members are first- or second-year undergraduate students. We use this time to build understanding and find topics that interest you.

Use this roadmap at a pace that fits your time and interests. You can watch more or fewer videos, or skip some. Some lectures depend on earlier material, so return to that material when you need the background.

The mentor will add materials and guidance for later weeks. Record other topics that you explore in your own mentee folder.

## Write, ask, and share

Add notes to your own folder in any format that works for you. Share what you learned and the questions you want to discuss with the group.

You do not need to summarize every part of a lecture or chapter. You can explain an idea in your own words, work through an example, or identify a point you do not understand. Include timestamps or page numbers to help others find the relevant passage.

A few thoughtful observations or questions are enough. Write from your own thinking, without using AI to generate your notes or make them longer. Do not submit AI-generated summaries as your own work.

## Sharing with the group

Link to your notes from your folder's `README.md`. To bring a question or observation to the group, open an issue or start a discussion with a link to your notes.
