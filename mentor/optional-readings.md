# Optional reading materials

The [shared roadmap](roadmap.md) is the group's common weekly schedule. If you would rather follow a different reading direction, propose to Ziyang what you would like to read from this list (or another source), and share notes from your own thinking in your [mentee folder](../mentees/).

- [*Algorithmic Game Theory*](https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/algorithmic-game-theory/0092C07CA8B724E1B1BE2238DDD66B38) — edited by Noam Nisan, Tim Roughgarden, Éva Tardos, and Vijay V. Vazirani. Look under the publisher's Resources tab for online access; check [U of T Libraries](https://library.utoronto.ca/use/tool/librarysearch) for a downloadable edition if needed.
- [Tim Roughgarden's Algorithmic Game Theory lectures](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLEGCF-WLh2RJBqmxvZ0_ie-mleCFhi2N4) — video lectures from Stanford's CS364A course.
- [*Computational Complexity: A Modern Approach*](https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/computational-complexity/3453CAFDEB0B4820B186FE69A64E1086) — Sanjeev Arora and Boaz Barak. The [free author draft](https://theory.cs.princeton.edu/complexity/) is fine, but prefer a published edition through U of T Libraries when available. The draft is marked preliminary, and [Arora notes](https://www.cs.princeton.edu/courses/archive/spring16/cos522/) that later printings corrected many typos in earlier printings.
- [Ryan O'Donnell's Graduate Complexity Theory at CMU lectures](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLm3J0oaFux3b8Gg1DdaJOzYNsaXYLAOKH) — video lectures from CMU's 15-855 course.
- [*A Graduate Course in Applied Cryptography*](https://toc.cryptobook.us/) — Dan Boneh and Victor Shoup.
- [*A Pragmatic Introduction to Secure Multi-Party Computation*](https://securecomputation.org/docs/pragmaticmpc.pdf) — David Evans, Vladimir Kolesnikov, and Mike Rosulek.
- [*A Proof of Security of Yao's Protocol for Two-Party Computation*](https://eprint.iacr.org/2004/175) — Yehuda Lindell and Benny Pinkas.
- [*How To Simulate It – A Tutorial on the Simulation Proof Technique*](https://eprint.iacr.org/2016/046.pdf) — Yehuda Lindell.
