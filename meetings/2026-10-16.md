## Finite fields

Ziyang gave a basic introduction to finite fields. A finite field is a finite set with addition, subtraction, multiplication, and division by nonzero elements. These operations obey the usual field rules. Finite fields provide a setting for arithmetic with secret shares.

## Additive secret sharing and the age-sum example

We discussed how three people can use additive secret sharing to compute the sum of their ages. Each person splits their input into random shares. Participants add the shares they hold, then combine the resulting shares to recover the total.

The [companion handout](../mentor/finite-fields-and-secret-sharing.md) gives a concrete version of the protocol, a worked numerical example, and the assumptions behind the privacy guarantee. The example distinguishes information revealed by the total from additional information that the protocol should keep private.
